# My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man

## Introduction
One of my favorite movies is **Spider-Man: No Way Home**. It is an action and science-fiction movie about responsibility, friendship, and sacrifice.

## Movie Information
- **Title:** Spider-Man: No Way Home
- **Genre:** Action, Adventure, Science Fiction
- **Release Year:** 2021
- **Main Character:** Peter Parker
- **Director:** Jon Watts

## Why I Like This Movie
I like this movie because it has an interesting story, exciting scenes, and meaningful lessons. It also shows how important it is to make responsible decisions.

## Favorite Characters
1. Peter Parker
2. Doctor Strange
3. MJ
4. Ned Leeds
5. Aunt May

## What I Learned
> With great power comes great responsibility.

The movie reminds me that every decision has consequences and that helping others is important.

## Conclusion
Overall, Spider-Man: No Way Home is one of my favorite movies because it combines entertainment with meaningful lessons about friendship, responsibility, and courage.
